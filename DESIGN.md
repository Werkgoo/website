# Werkgo candidate landing page

## Direction

Replace the quiet centered layout with an editorial split hero. Oversized Manrope type carries the job seeker's ambition, while a large worksite portrait introduces the people and trades. Palette: cobalt #2449e8, ink #142134, paper #faf9f5, pale blue #edf0fc, white #ffffff. The existing orange workwear supplies the warm accent through photography rather than decorative UI.

The new outlined lowercase wordmark has a forward arrow instead of a capsule. Hero, full-width reassurance strip, image-led work-area selection, CV intake, process, large statement, FAQ and final CTA establish distinct levels without adding another offer.

## Interaction

One primary action, upload a CV. Sector links keep selecting the matching form field. The two-step form and error handling are preserved. Reduced motion disables the image entrance and word reveal; essential content is always visible. Mobile reflows the hero with the CTA before the portrait.

## Production work still required

Netlify Forms receipt, suitable CV storage and access controls, legal controller details, retention policy and Google Ads conversion measurement remain production tasks. No fictional ratings, testimonials or vacancy counts are used.
